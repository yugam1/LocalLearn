# Beacon — Plans & Pricing

Beacon is billed per active Beacon Tag per month. An "active" Tag is one that
reported at least one ping in the billing month; a Tag that never reports is not
billed. Billing is monthly and prorated to the day a Tag is activated.

## Plan tiers

| Plan       | Price / Tag / mo | Max Tags | History retention | Webhooks | Support SLA        |
|------------|------------------|----------|-------------------|----------|--------------------|
| Starter    | $4               | 25       | 6 months          | No       | Email, 48h         |
| Pro        | $9               | 250      | 18 months         | Yes      | Email, 8h          |
| Enterprise | Custom           | Unlimited| 5 years           | Yes      | 24/7 phone, 1h     |

Note: the **Max Tags** column is a hard cap. Once you hit it, new Tags will
report but stay in a "pending" state and are not shown on the map until you
upgrade or remove existing Tags. Geofence count is unlimited on every plan.

## Overages and downgrades

There are no per-ping overage charges — the plan price is all-in for reporting.
If you downgrade while over the new plan's Max Tags cap, the most recently
activated Tags are moved to "pending" first (newest deactivated first) until you
are back under the cap. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing
month; upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated.

## Billing contact

Only an account Owner can change the plan or payment method, from
**Console → Settings → Billing**. Invoices are emailed on the 1st of each month.
